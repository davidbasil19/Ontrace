Privacy

Short version: OnTrace doesn't collect your personal data.

  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies set by OnTrace.
  • Local storage only: your watchlist, custom RPC address, intro preference and alert progress are saved in your own browser's storage. You can clear them anytime by clearing site data.
  • Public blockchain data: wallet addresses you scan, watch or connect are sent to a Solana RPC provider (and token addresses to DexScreener and Jupiter) to fetch public information. Those providers may log requests under their own privacy policies.
