Security

OnTrace is a wallet tool, so security is the product. Here's exactly what it can and can't do.

OnTrace will never ask for your seed phrase, DM you first, or ask you to "validate", "sync" or "recover" your wallet. Our only official places are ontrace.space, @0ntrace on X and team@ontrace.space.

What OnTrace can do

  • Read public blockchain data — balances, token accounts, token settings and transaction history.
  • Read public market data from DexScreener and swap quotes from Jupiter.
  • Ask your wallet to sign transactions that you start. Your wallet shows each one for approval.

What OnTrace cannot do

  • See, store or ask for your seed phrase or private key.
  • Move funds without your wallet's approval.
  • Sign anything in the background.
  • Track you — there is no server, database, account system or analytics.

Exactly what each action does

ActionWhat gets sent to Solana
Scan, check, watch, trace, inspectNothing — read-only, no signature
Reclaim SOLClose account, only on empty token accounts you own
Burn junkBurn + close account, only on tokens you tick
Revoke approvalsRevoke, only on approvals you tick
Unwrap SOLClose your wrapped-SOL account
SwapThe swap transaction Jupiter builds for the quote you saw
Multi-sendTransfers to the addresses you entered (plus creating their token accounts if needed)
Create tokenCreate the token, mint the supply to you, and optionally lock supply / metadata. No freeze authority is ever set.

Cleanup instructions were verified byte-for-byte against Solana's official token library.

Built-in protections

  • Confirmation step before burns, multi-sends and token creation.
  • Risk acknowledgement before swapping into tokens rated High risk or Likely scam.
  • Drainer detection in the Transaction inspector: it flags ownership hand-overs, new spending approvals, accounts closed to someone else and unknown programs.
  • Safe display: token names from outside sources are cleaned before showing, so a malicious token name can't inject anything into the page.
  • Local-only storage: your watchlist and settings never leave your browser.

Open source

The full site is published on GitHub. What you read there is what runs here. The detailed technical security notes are in SECURITY.md.

Staying safe anywhere on Solana

  • Read what your wallet shows before you approve anything.
  • Ignore airdropped tokens that link to "claim" sites — they're usually drainers. Burn them with OnTrace.
  • Check unknown tokens with the Token checker before buying.
  • Revoke approvals you don't recognise.
  • Use a separate wallet for trying new apps.

Report a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately to team@ontrace.space with a description, steps to reproduce and any transaction signatures or screenshots. Please don't post security issues publicly. We aim to reply within 72 hours.
