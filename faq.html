Basics

What is OnTrace?

OnTrace helps you understand your wallet, investigate what you don't trust, and take control of your on-chain activity on Solana. It shows what's really in a wallet and which tokens carry red flags; explains transactions, token developers and wallet relationships; and lets you clean up, swap, send and create tokens — with every transaction safety-checked.

Is it free?

OnTrace charges no platform fees. Standard Solana network fees apply when you send a transaction (usually a fraction of a cent), plus an optional priority fee you can set in Settings. Some actions also need refundable account deposits (rent), e.g. creating a token.

Do I need to set anything up?

No. OnTrace has a built-in connection to Solana. Just open the site — connect a wallet only when you want to take an action. (Advanced users can switch to their own RPC provider in Settings.)

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Connect your wallet when you want to take an action, or paste any address for a read-only scan.

Which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare and Backpack. Other wallets that inject a standard Solana provider may also work.

Who runs OnTrace?

An independent team. Reach us on X at @0ntrace or by email at team@ontrace.space.

Safety

Can OnTrace take my funds?

No. OnTrace can only ask your wallet to sign a transaction you started. Before it even asks, its built-in transaction guard simulates the transaction and blocks it if it would hand over your tokens, add an approval, send tokens you didn't ask to send, or spend unexpected SOL. Your wallet then shows the transaction for your final approval. OnTrace never sees your seed phrase or private key.

Will OnTrace ever ask for my seed phrase?

Never. Anyone asking for your seed phrase — including someone claiming to be OnTrace support — is trying to steal from you.

Is the code public?

Yes. The entire site is on GitHub, so anyone can check exactly what it does. What you see there is what runs at ontrace.space.

What data do you collect?

None. OnTrace has no server, database or analytics. Your watchlist and settings are stored only in your own browser. See Privacy.

How do I know I'm on the real site?

Check the address bar says ontrace.space with a padlock. Bookmark it. Our only accounts are @0ntrace on X and team@ontrace.space.

Tools

What does "Reclaim SOL" do?

Every token account on Solana holds a small deposit (about 0.002 SOL). When an account is empty, that SOL just sits there. OnTrace closes empty accounts and sends the deposits back to you. Accounts with tokens in them are never touched. Some wallets have hundreds of empty accounts — we've seen over 1.6 SOL in a single wallet.

How are tokens rated?

OnTrace combines live market data (liquidity, market cap, trading activity, age) with the token's on-chain settings (whether the creator can freeze your tokens, mint more, or move tokens out of wallets). Ratings run from No major red flags to Likely scam, with the reasons listed. Full rules are on GitHub.

Does "No major red flags" mean a token is safe?

No. It means none of OnTrace's automated checks found a problem. Passing automated checks doesn't make a token safe — scams can still slip through, and good projects can still fail. Ratings aren't financial advice. Always do your own research.

What happens when I burn a token?

The tokens are destroyed permanently and the account's deposit is returned to you. OnTrace shows the estimated value being destroyed and asks you to confirm first. It can't be undone.

Where do swaps come from?

Swaps are routed by Jupiter, which finds the best price across Solana's exchanges. OnTrace checks the token you're buying and warns you if it looks risky.

Can I create a token that people trust?

Yes. Tokens made with OnTrace have no freeze authority (nobody can freeze holders), and you can lock the supply so no more can ever be minted. To make it tradeable you'll still need to add a liquidity pool on Raydium or Meteora.

How do alerts work?

Add wallets to your Watchlist and press Start alerts. OnTrace checks them every 30 seconds and notifies you of buys, sells, swaps or big moves. Alerts run while OnTrace is open in a browser tab.

What is the Transaction inspector?

Paste a transaction signature to see what happened in plain English, or paste an unsigned transaction to simulate it before signing. It flags wallet-drainer tricks like handing over ownership of your token accounts.

Mobile

How do I use OnTrace on my phone?

Phone wallets connect inside their own built-in browser. Open ontrace.space and tap Connect wallet — you'll get buttons to open OnTrace inside Phantom or Solflare. Or open your wallet app, find its browser (Phantom: the compass/browser icon; Solflare: Explore) and go to ontrace.space.

Troubleshooting

My wallet won't connect.
  • Make sure your wallet extension is installed and unlocked, then reload.
  • On a phone, open OnTrace inside your wallet's browser (see Mobile).
  • Check the network menu: use Mainnet for real funds, Devnet only for testing.
A transaction failed.

Usually the network was busy. Try again, or raise the priority fee in Settings. If you were swapping a fast-moving token, increase slippage or refresh the quote.

Something looks wrong.

Email team@ontrace.space with a screenshot and, if possible, the transaction signature. For security issues, see Security.
